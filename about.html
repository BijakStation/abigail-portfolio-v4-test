Abigail Diantete

Abigail Diantete is a Paris-based stylist working across fashion, editorial and visual storytelling. Graduated in Fashion Communication and Management, she has developed her experience across style and image, working with creative teams, brands and artists.

Her experience in styling, production coordination and talent management allows her to approach a project from both a creative and organisational perspective — from developing an initial idea and moodboard to coordinating the people, garments and details that bring it to life.

Selected services

  • Editorial and Campaign
  • Image Consulting
  • Creative Direction
  • Wardrobe Styling
